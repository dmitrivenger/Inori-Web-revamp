FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a Digital Payment Note (DPN)?

A DPN is a digital record of a payer's commitment to pay a supplier a set amount on a set date. It is issued and tracked on a blockchain ledger, so ownership and history are always verifiable.

How does a supplier get paid early?

Once the supplier holds a DPN, it can sell the note to a participating funder and receive payment before the due date. At maturity, the payer pays the funder.

Does the payer have to change its payment terms?

No. The payer keeps its existing terms and pays on the original due date — to whoever holds the note at that time.

What happens after I leave my details?

Is my inquiry confidential?

Yes. All inquiries are handled in full confidence and nothing is shared with any party without your explicit consent.
