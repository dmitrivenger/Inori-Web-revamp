For buyers

Keep your payment date. Give suppliers a choice.

Reverse factoring starts with something both of you already agree on: an approved invoice. When you approve it, NEVO records it as a DPN with a clear buyer, supplier, amount and due date. If INORI pays the supplier early, the DPN moves to INORI, so on the due date you pay INORI instead. Same amount, same date.

Your date stays the same

You never pay early. You pay on the due date you approved.

Approve it once

The amount and due date you confirm stay fixed in the shared record.

Always know who to pay

The record shows who owns each DPN right now and where the payment should go.

Happier suppliers

Your suppliers get an optional way to access their money sooner.

What you do

  1. Approve the supplier's invoice, with the amount and due date.
  2. NEVO issues the DPN to the supplier.
  3. On the due date, pay whoever owns the DPN, following the instructions.
