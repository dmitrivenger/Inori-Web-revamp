For suppliers

Your approved payments, available when you need them.

Waiting 60, 90 or 120 days for payment slows growth. With DPN, an approved payment becomes a digital note you can turn into cash — backed by your customer's commitment, not by your own balance sheet.

Get paid early

Sell your note to a participating funder and receive cash before the due date.

Clear and verifiable

Amount, due date and payer are recorded on the ledger — no disputes about what was approved.

Plan your growth

Predictable cash flow helps you take on new orders and projects.

Your choice

Decide per note whether to wait or receive early payment.

Your role in the flow

  1. Deliver and invoice your customer as usual.
  2. Receive a DPN once the payment is approved.
  3. Hold it, or sell it to a funder for early payment.
