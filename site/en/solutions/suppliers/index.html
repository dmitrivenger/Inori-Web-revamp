For suppliers

Your work is done. Why wait months to get paid?

You've already done the work. NEVO lets you turn an approved invoice into a clear request for early payment. You see the terms before you decide, and you can follow every step until the money arrives and the invoice is settled. Funding is optional and depends on INORI's review and the agreed terms.

It's your call

Rather wait? Keep the DPN until the due date.

What you do

  1. Add the invoice. Once your buyer approves it, a DPN is issued to you.
  2. Choose whether to wait for the due date or ask INORI to pay early.
  3. If it's approved and you accept the terms, INORI pays you by bank transfer.
