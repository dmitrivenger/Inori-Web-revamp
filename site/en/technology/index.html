Technology

Enterprise blockchain, designed for finance.

DPN runs on Hyperledger Iroha — a permissioned distributed ledger built for regulated financial infrastructure.

The ledger

Why a distributed ledger?

Factoring depends on trust: that a payment was approved, that a note exists only once, and that its owner is who they claim to be. A shared ledger gives every participant the same verified record — without relying on emails, spreadsheets or paper.

Shared record

All authorised parties see the same state of every note.

Tamper-proof

History cannot be altered after the fact.

Instant transfer

Ownership moves between parties in a single recorded step.

DLT

Hyperledger Iroha

An open-source, permissioned blockchain under the Linux Foundation's Hyperledger project, designed for digital assets, identity and payment systems in regulated environments.

Strategic partnership

In partnership with Soramitsu

INORI is the official Israeli partner of Soramitsu, a leading developer of blockchain-based financial infrastructure and central bank digital currency systems, and a core developer of Hyperledger Iroha. The partnership brings proven technology and international standards for security, privacy and interoperability to the Israeli market.
