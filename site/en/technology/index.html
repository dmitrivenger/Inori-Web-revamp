Technology

Solid technology behind every payment.

NEVO records the state and ownership of every DPN on SORA Nexus, built on Hyperledger Iroha v3.

What the ledger does

One record everyone can follow

Authorised users can follow the invoice, approvals, funding status and payment instructions in one place. The ledger keeps the full history of each DPN's state and ownership, while the money itself moves through banks.

One approved obligation

The amount, the parties, their consent, the due date and the status stay linked together.

A clear ownership history

Each transfer is recorded once INORI has checked the bank evidence and signed it.

Proof of payment

Payment proof is matched to the transaction, so everyone with access can see when it's settled.

DLT

SORA Nexus and Hyperledger Iroha v3

Hyperledger Iroha is open-source distributed ledger technology, and Soramitsu has played a central role in building it. NEVO uses SORA Nexus, built on Iroha v3, as the ledger for DPNs. You never have to deal with the blockchain directly: companies simply work in a web portal.

Strategic partnership

INORI represents Soramitsu in Israel

Soramitsu is a Japanese fintech company, founded in Tokyo in 2016, that builds blockchain-based solutions for digital assets, digital identity and financial infrastructure. Together with the National Bank of Cambodia, it developed and runs Bakong, Cambodia's national blockchain-based payment system. INORI represents Soramitsu in Israel.
